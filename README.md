## 📖 About the Project

Hey, I'm **haymooed**! 👋 

**MarketDex** is my custom Discord bot and collectible card ecosystem built from the ground up to take the classic ball-dex formula to the next level. Featuring custom-designed **Marketballs**, an active player-driven trading hub, custom card crafting, and live token economies, MarketDex turns every Discord conversation into an exciting trading floor. I love Marvel Rivals and Fortnite also. 

Whether you're an avid collector hunting down rare shiny specials or a merchant looking to dominate the auction house, there's always something new dropping in the market.

---

## 🌟 What Makes MarketDex Special

* 💎 **Exclusive Marketball Roster**: Over 150+ custom pixel-art and illustrated Marketballs to discover, catch, and inspect.
* 🪙 **Living Token Economy**: Earn Market Tokens on every catch, trade them with other merchants, or buy rare items in rotating shops.
* 📊 **Modern Dashboard UI**: Interactive Discord Components V2 interface with real-time completion tracking, wallet summaries, and a full emoji codex.
* 🤝 **Live Trading & Auctions**: Seamless peer-to-peer trading with cooldown protection, plus real-time player auction bidding.
